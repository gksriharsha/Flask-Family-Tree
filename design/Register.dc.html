Register

Not a chart at all. The generation in focus is set as a page from a family register: the couple as a heading, their children as numbered entries beneath it. Descent is carried by the heading and the numbering, so no lines are drawn.

What it buys. Ten siblings cost ten rows instead of a canvas three screens wide, the birth order is stated rather than implied by position, and every name gets its full width — so no more truncated cards. It is also the only one of these that would print.

The trade, and it is a real one. This stops being a tree. The left-to-right shape that makes a family chart readable at a glance is gone, and with it the sense of a line running down the page. It would want to be a view you switch to, beside Lineage and View all, rather than a replacement for either.
