The tile carries the whole burden at small sizes, so the glyph inside can stay the mark already in the top bar rather than becoming a different animal. The lower leaf keeps #7FA352, the second olive the top-bar mark already uses, so the favicon and the mark are the same two colours rather than merely similar. It is the closest of the tints to the tile, so the lower leaf softens at 16px and the icon reads as one leaf and a stem at that size — a lighter sage separates them more, at the cost of a colour the brand does not otherwise contain.
