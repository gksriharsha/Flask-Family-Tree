How faces are handled Boxes are drawn in your browser and never leave it as anything but coordinates. No face measurement, template or embedding is computed or stored — only the box someone drew and the name they chose, with a note of who tagged it and when. Tagging a living relative needs their agreement first, and any tag can be removed by the person in it.
