Family vocabulary

The same relation is called different things depending on who is speaking. Your father’s people say చిన్నాన్న; your mother’s say కక్కయ్య. Neither is a correction of the other. Set the words each side actually uses, and the tree speaks in them.

These are suggestions, not authority. They are the forms in common use, offered as a starting point — the family is the authority on its own words, so every one of them can be replaced. Nothing here changes a single relationship: the tree works out who someone is from the parent and union links, and this page only decides what that gets called. That is why a word can be changed, or a whole regional vocabulary swapped, without touching the data.
